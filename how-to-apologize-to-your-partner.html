How to apologize to your partner so it actually lands

A good apology is short, specific and has no "but" in it. Here is a simple way to do it when you are nervous and want to get it right.

Name exactly what you did

"I am sorry if you were hurt" puts the problem on their reaction. Say what you did instead: "I am sorry I interrupted you and made a joke when you were telling me something serious."

Say how it affected them

Show you understand the effect, not just the act. "I can see that left you feeling small in front of our friends." If you are not sure, ask: "How did that feel for you?"

Drop the "but"

Everything after "but" cancels what you said before it. If you have your own side, save it for later, after they feel heard.

Say what you will do next time

One clear change is better than a big promise. "Next time I will wait until you finish before I speak."

Give them room

You are not owed forgiveness right away. Let them have the time they need and follow through on the change.
