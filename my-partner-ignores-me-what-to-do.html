My partner ignores me: what to do (and say)

Feeling ignored hurts, and the usual reaction, pushing harder or going cold, makes it worse. This is a calmer way to bring it up.

Check what "ignoring" means here

Sometimes it is distraction, tiredness or a phone. Sometimes it is avoidance. The fix is different, so get curious before you decide it is deliberate.

Pick a calm time

Not in the middle of a fight, and not at bedtime. Try: "Can we talk for ten minutes after dinner? Nothing is wrong. I want us to feel closer."

Use one moment and one feeling

"When I was telling you about my day and you kept scrolling, I felt unimportant." It is hard to argue with, because it is your experience.

Ask for one small thing

"Could we put phones away during dinner?" A small, clear request is easier to say yes to than a big complaint.

Notice the response

Give it a few weeks. If you keep asking kindly and nothing changes, that is information, and it may be time for a counselor.
