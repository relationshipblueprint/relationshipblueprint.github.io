How to stop fighting with your partner and end the same fight for good

Couples rarely fight about the dishes. They fight about feeling unseen, unfair or alone. Here is how to break the loop.

Spot your pattern

Most people have a default: fix it, go quiet, smooth it over or argue it. Knowing yours helps you catch it in the moment. The quiz on this site is a quick way to find out.

Agree on a pause word

Pick a word or phrase either of you can use when it is heating up. It means: stop, breathe, and come back in 20 minutes. The one who asks for the pause is the one who restarts the talk.

Start soft

How a talk starts usually decides how it ends. "I feel worried about us and I would like to talk" beats "You never listen".

Repair quickly

A small repair, such as "that came out harsher than I meant", can stop a bad moment from becoming a bad night.
