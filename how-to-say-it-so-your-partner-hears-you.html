How to say hard things so your partner can hear them

The same message can start a fight or a conversation. The difference is usually in how you open it.

Lead with what you want, not what they did wrong

"I would like us to share the evening chores more evenly" lands better than "You never help".

Be specific

"Always" and "never" invite a debate about whether they are true. A single recent example is harder to dispute.

Say the feeling in one word

Hurt, worried, lonely, overwhelmed. One honest word does more than a paragraph of reasons.

Then listen

Ask: "What is this like for you?" and say back what you heard before you respond.
